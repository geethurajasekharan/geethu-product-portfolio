AI-native healthcare coordination

An AI-powered personal health command center for patients and caregivers — making healthcare management simple, proactive, and accessible.

My Health Node AI helps users manage medications, refills, appointments, lab reports, caregiver follow-ups, and daily health tasks in one place. The product is designed for everyday patients and families who struggle to coordinate care across pharmacies, doctors, labs, portals, paper documents, phone calls, and caregivers. It uses AI, OCR, structured workflows, voice calling, reminders, and trusted health content to help users stay organized and close the loop on important care tasks.


It does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend treatment changes, or independently order tests. It helps patients and caregivers organize documented health information, understand it in plain language, and act on confirmed next steps.

Competitive analysis

Navigating the competitive landscape

My Health Node AI offers a unique blend of AI-powered intelligence and caregiver-first design, setting it apart from existing solutions. We analysed key competitors to identify our strategic advantages and market opportunities.

Where competitors stop

Existing tools reduce to reminders and record storage — they tell a family what to do, but never confirm whether it happened.

Where we fill the gap

Intelligent, automated support that goes beyond simple reminders — comprehensive care coordination with actionable insights and alerts for patient and caregiver alike.

AI hypothesis

Closing the loop when a dose is missed

One core hypothesis, tested down three layers — behaviour, engagement, and revenue.

Core hypothesis

If I deliver automated medication tracking and closed-loop escalation when a medication is missed with greater than 95% accuracy to family caregivers of older adults, the caregiver is in a position to ensure medications are taken on time and act quickly when they are not, which improves adherence and prevents care gaps, which increases caregiver trust and engagement with My Health Node.

Monetization hypothesis

Caregivers who actively monitor medication adherence and receive at least one meaningful intervention opportunity during their first month are more likely to perceive ongoing value and convert to a paid subscription at the end of a 30-day free trial.
