Attuned AI is an AI-powered parenting companion that helps parents navigate emotionally difficult moments with greater awareness, empathy, and confidence. Built on interviews with 10+ parents and survey research with 40+ respondents, it combines real-time AI coaching, child behaviour insights, parent growth reflections, and personalized storytelling — powered by RAG, long-term family memory, and agentic AI workflows that grow alongside the parent and child.

Our AI hypothesis

Helping parents respond, not react.

Everything in Attuned AI was built around a single behavioural hypothesis.

If the AI delivers warm, age-appropriate, NVC-framed responses with less than 15% age-stage misattribution to parents of children ages 2–12, they are able to recognize their own emotional triggers and respond to their child in the moment rather than react, which improves D7 session return rate — with 3+ sessions in the first 7 days predicting conversion — ultimately increasing free-to-paid conversion within the first week.

Why it mattered

Trust depended on developmental accuracy. If guidance felt too mature for a toddler or too childish for a pre-teen, parents were less likely to trust the recommendation, return to the product, or convert.

What it unlocked

Turning developmental appropriateness into a measurable quality metric transformed AI trust from a subjective concern into a product KPI tied directly to retention, engagement, and conversion.
